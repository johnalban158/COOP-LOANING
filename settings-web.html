📖 How to Use CampusCoop
Step-by-step guide for administrators. Click each section to expand.
🏠 Dashboard▶

The Dashboard is your home screen. It shows at-a-glance stats: total members, active loans, pending applications, and collection summary.

  • Check pending applications to approve or reject loan requests.
  • Review overdue accounts that need follow-up.
  • Monitor collection progress for the current month.
👥 Managing Members▶
  • Go to Members page to see all registered cooperative members.
  • To add a new member, go to the Login page → "Add Member" tab. Fill in name, email, phone, department, and password.
  • Each member gets an auto-generated Member Number (e.g. 1001, 1002...).
  • Members log in using their Member Number + Password.
  • Click View on any member row to see their full profile and loan eligibility.
💰 Loan Types & Applications▶

CampusCoop supports 3 loan types:

  • Short Term Loan — Max ₱15,000, 1-6 months, 6% flat interest. Available to all members.
  • Regular Loan — Max ₱200,000 (or 3× capital), 1-5 years, 1% diminishing interest. Teachers only.
  • Commodity Loan — Max ₱5,000, 1-2 months, 1% interest/month. For purchasing goods.

Members submit applications from their dashboard. Go to Applications to approve, reject, or review.

💳 Recording & Confirming Payments▶
  • Go to Payments to see all submitted payments.
  • Members can submit payments with proof of payment (screenshot/photo) from their own dashboard.
  • You (admin) can Confirm a payment — this generates an Official Receipt (OR) and deducts the balance from their loan.
  • You can also Record a payment manually on behalf of a member (e.g. counter payment).
  • Use Reject if the payment is invalid, with a reason.
📈 Collections, Overdue & Reports▶
  • Collections — Shows monthly collection summary grouped by loan type.
  • Overdue — Lists all members with past-due payments. You can send reminders from here.
  • Reports — Generate weekly/monthly reports with totals and breakdowns.
  • Reminders (SMS) — Send payment reminders via SMS to members with upcoming or overdue payments.
⚙️ Settings & Admin Access▶
  • Database connection — Paste your Google Spreadsheet ID to connect data storage.
  • School year — Update the academic year label shown across the system.
  • Admin password — Change login credentials for the admin dashboard.
  • Admin login — Use username ICMADMIN and your set password to access the admin dashboard.
